Mochi’s Legacy

Boulder-area volunteer dogs and handlers sharing comfort and joy.

Mochi was a very beloved Golden Retriever who lived in Boulder, CO. Her sweet, playful, affectionate, and loving personality were unique and touched the hearts and lives of many people. Mochi had an ability to quietly connect with people and calm them. She exuded unconditional love. People often asked if she was a therapy dog. Officially she was not. In practice, however, this seems to have been her life’s mission, and she fulfilled it beautifully. She crossed the rainbow bridge in July, 2026 just weeks before her 13th birthday. The mission of Mochi’s Legacy Golden Retriever Therapy nonprofit is to continue Mochi’s beautiful legacy of bringing love and healing to others. Mochi’s Legacy works with Golden Retrievers whose humans volunteer them to: 1 Go into the Boulder community to schools, hospitals, and elder care facilities 2 Participate in our regular “Gaggle of Goldens” walks around Boulder with up to 15 Golden Retrievers at a time, brightening people’s days and promoting our nonprofit 3 Be available for people to participate in Golden Retriever experiences on a Boulder farm several times a month (starting in early 2027)

How vetting works

  1. 1
    Gaggle

    Founder-approved, people- and dog-friendly dogs start with walks.

  2. 2
    Candidate

    A founder reviews walks, behaviour and opt-outs before suitable visits.

  3. 3
    Therapy

    Registry-certified. Founders record certification; we don’t certify dogs.

Founders decide with evidence. Never automatic.

Therapy dogs are not service dogs.

Our Board

Our board

  • Ed Bardzik

    President and Founder · Chief Mochi Officer

    Ed has loved dogs ever since his family picked out a Cocker Spaniel when he was 5 years old. He has had a longtime affinity for Golden Retrievers. Mochi came into his life through a chance meeting at Kiln in 2022. He was inspired by the way Mochi touched the hearts and lives of so many people with her sweet, playful, affectionate, and loving spirit. Mochi was an integral part of how his friendships with Dylan and Sarah (see bios below) developed.

    A lifelong entrepreneur, Ed owns Respect Inc., an HR services company, and is a Colorado residential real estate agent. Ed is excited to delve into the nonprofit world and to see Mochi’s Legacy continue through this organization named in her honor.

  • Dylan Agema

    Treasurer · Gaggle of Goldens Guru

    Dylan has spent the last 5 years dog-sitting across the US, with the last year spent full-time in Boulder. Ed and Dylan were drawn together through their mutual love of dogs, with Dylan’s revolving door of dogs being warmly received by Ed, Mochi, and the Kiln community. From February 2026 onwards, Dylan was lucky enough to befriend Mochi and see the lasting effect she had on people anywhere she went. Dylan helped build the Mochi’s Legacy website and is enthusiastically involved in taking photos and managing the gaggle of goldens initiative to bring Boulder’s large community of dog-loving individuals together.

  • Nolan Bonnie

    Secretary · Chief Wag Officer

    Nolan is currently a Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Colorado Boulder. A longtime dog lover, Nolan’s family dog in California is a beautiful 14 year-old Lab named Stella who has brought much joy to his life. Nolan met Ed and Mochi on the pickleball court in 2024 and he and Mochi formed a tight bond. In February, Nolan attended Goldens in Golden with Ed, Mochi, and Sarah. As the idea for Mochi’s Legacy was coming together, Nolan suggested that Mochi’s Legacy procure a booth at Goldens in Golden in 2027. He not only championed that effort; he also worked on website setup and is advising on the process of certifying the Golden Retrievers in our program to be able to work in schools.

  • Sarah Montgomery

    Advisor · Golden Advisor

    Sarah and Ed literally met because of Mochi. Ed was walking Mochi through the kitchen at Kiln in February 2025, and Sarah commented on what a beautiful dog she was and asked Ed what her name was. When Ed said “Mochi”, Sarah responded that she is an aunt to another Mochi, a male miniature Golden Doodle. Ed, Sarah, both Mochis and Nolan all attended Goldens in Golden earlier this year. Sarah helped Ed to choose the official name of the Mochi’s Legacy Golden Retriever Therapy, and she is instrumental in helping our nonprofit form alliances with companies like King Soopers.
